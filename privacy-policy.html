Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us

Last updated: August 2026

KDST Real Estate ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

When you fill out our contact form, we collect the information you provide directly: your name, email address, phone number, and any details about the property you're interested in. We do not collect payment information, and we do not run advertising or retargeting campaigns.

Analytics

This site is built to work with Google Analytics, but analytics is not currently active — no tracking script is loaded and no visitor data is collected for analytics purposes at this time. If we enable Google Analytics in the future, this section will be updated first to explain what's collected (typically aggregated traffic data such as which pages are viewed and roughly how visitors found us, not anything that identifies you personally) and how to opt out, for example via Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

How We Use It

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry — whether that's answering a question about a listing, scheduling a visit, or following up on a purchase. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Store It

Contact form submissions are currently delivered to our team's email inbox. If we start using a third-party form-processing service to handle submissions instead, we'll name that processor here and describe how long it retains your information. We keep inquiry records only as long as needed to assist you with your property search, and we take reasonable steps to keep that information secure.

Property Listings & Documentation

Where property listings reference ownership documents, survey maps, or title records, that information is used only to demonstrate our verification process to prospective buyers and is handled with the same care as any other client-related paperwork.

Your Rights

You can ask us to correct or delete information you've submitted at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Contact Us
