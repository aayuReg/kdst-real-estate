Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to purchase land?

You'll need citizenship certificate, passport-size photos, and PAN card. We'll guide you through the complete documentation process.

Do you verify all properties?

Yes, absolutely. We conduct thorough verification of land titles, ownership documents, and legal clearances before listing any property.

Can I get bank financing?

Yes, we have partnerships with major banks. We can help you connect with financial institutions and assist with the loan process.

What areas do you cover?

We primarily operate in Kathmandu Valley, Bhaktapur, Sunsari, and Itahari regions, with expanding coverage across Nepal.

How long does the buying process take?

Typically 2-4 weeks from agreement to final registration, depending on documentation and legal processes.

Do you charge commission?

Yes, standard industry commission applies. We're transparent about all costs upfront with no hidden fees.
