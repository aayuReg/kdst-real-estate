About KDST Real Estate

Building trust and creating opportunities in Nepal's real estate market

Your Trusted Real Estate Partner

KDST Real Estate has been serving the Nepal community for over a decade, specializing in land and housing transactions across Kathmandu Valley, Sunsari, and Itahari regions. Our mission is to make property ownership accessible, transparent, and secure for every Nepali family.

We understand that purchasing land or a home is one of life's most significant investments. That's why we go beyond simple transactions to provide comprehensive support, from property verification and legal documentation to post-purchase assistance.

Our team of experienced professionals brings deep local knowledge, strong ethical values, and a commitment to excellence in every deal we facilitate. We don't just sell properties – we build lasting relationships with our clients.

Our Core Values

Trust & Integrity

We operate with complete transparency and honesty. Every property we represent undergoes rigorous verification, and we provide all documentation upfront so you can make informed decisions.

Client-First Approach

Your needs and goals drive everything we do. We take time to understand your requirements, budget, and vision to find properties that truly match your aspirations.

Professional Excellence

Our team consists of licensed professionals with extensive knowledge of Nepal's real estate market, legal requirements, and local regulations. We handle every detail with precision.

Community Growth

We're committed to developing sustainable communities. Our colony projects include proper infrastructure, utilities, and amenities that enhance quality of life.

Efficient Service
